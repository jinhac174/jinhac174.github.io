About

Undergraduate Research Intern @ Yonsei University / Senior Student @ Yonsei University

Research Intern in AI Graduate School

I am Jinha Choi, working as a research intern at DILLAB (Yonsei Univ) advised by Prof. Jongmin Lee. My current research focuses on action constrained imitation learning, where the agent learns from dataset with less or no action constraints. I aim to expand the topic to work on online reinforcement learning and robot learning.

I previously worked on humanoid loco-manipulation and generalizable humanoid at RLLAB (Yonsei Univ) advised by Prof. Youngwoon Lee.
