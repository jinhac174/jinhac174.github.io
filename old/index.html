Research intern in AI graduate school

I am Jinha Choi, working as a research intern at DILLAB (Yonsei Univ) advised by prof. Jongmin Lee. I previously worked on humanoid loco-manipulation and generalizable humanoid tasks. My current research focuses on action constrained imitation learning. I aim to continue working on online reinforcement learning and robot learning.

Background

I was born in Aug 22, 2002 in Seoul, South Korea. I graduated elementary school with some experience in robotics and lots of experience and training in soccer, then I moved to Vancouver, Canada in Aug 2014. I continued to play soccer for all the years until highschool graduation, winning several leagues and tournaments as a key player. In 2017, I joined Vex Robotics program supported in West Vancouver Secondary School. As the team lead, I managed designing, engineering, building, wiring, programming, and driving. Winnig several regionals and provincials (British Columbia, Canada), I qualified to World Championship held yearly in Kentucky, which hosts 600 teams from around the world. I placed around 32nd in the tournament, and it was a great experience.

I completed International Baccalaureate Diploma program at WVSS, and graduated highschool at WVSS. In 2020, I returned to Seoul, South Korea, and began my undergraduate program at Yonsei University in 2021. I completed my mandatory military service at Korea Defense Intelligence Command during 2023~2024, and now I am back at Yonsei, working as a research intern.
